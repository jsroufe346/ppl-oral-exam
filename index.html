Private Pilot Checkride Flight Tasks

A flight-task study checklist based on the Private Pilot Airplane ACS. The examiner selects tasks under the ACS; be ready to demonstrate each applicable task, not just memorize tolerances.

IV. Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds

TaskACSKey standards to study
Normal takeoff and climbPA.IV.APOH: flaps UP-10 (10 preferred); lift nosewheel at 55 KIAS, climb at 70-80 KIAS, and retract flaps at a safe altitude. Maintain centerline and assigned heading. POH 4-18
Soft-field takeoff and climbPA.IV.CPOH: 10° flaps; lift off as soon as practical, slightly tail-low. With no obstacle, level off to accelerate; keep 10° until obstacles are clear and at least 60 KIAS before retracting. For aft-CG loading, trim slightly nose-down for comfortable initial-climb forces. POH 4-31
Soft-field approach and landingPA.IV.DPOH landing guidance: power as needed for a stabilized approach; main wheels touch first, power idle at touchdown, then lower the nosewheel gently after speed decreases to avoid nose-gear loads. No separate soft-field approach speed is specified. POH 4-45
Short-field takeoff and maximum-performance climbPA.IV.EPOH: 10° flaps, hold brakes until full power, then release; slightly tail-low; 56 KIAS until obstacles are clear; retract flaps slowly above 60 KIAS. Use computed takeoff distance for actual conditions. POH 4-18
Short-field approach and landingPA.IV.FPOH: full flaps, 61 KIAS until flare, enough power to control glide path; reduce to idle after obstacles clear. Main wheels first, then heavy braking as required and flaps UP. Use a slightly higher speed in turbulence. POH 4-21, 4-45
Forward slip to a landingPA.IV.MUse the slip to manage the approach path and meet the ACS touchdown standard. POH notes that steep slips with more than 20° flaps may cause slight elevator oscillation under some conditions. POH 4-45
Go-around / rejected landingPA.IV.NPOH balked landing: full power, flaps to 20°, climb at 60 KIAS; if clearing obstacles, use 10° until clear, then retract at a safe altitude after reaching 65 KIAS. Maintain directional control. POH 4-22, 4-46
Normal approach and landingPA.IV.BPOH: 65-75 KIAS flaps UP; 60-70 KIAS with FULL flaps. Flap extension limits: 10° below 110 KIAS; above 10° through FULL below 85 KIAS. Touch down main wheels first, lower nosewheel gently, and use minimum braking required. POH 4-3, 4-21, 4-45

V. Performance and Ground Reference Maneuvers

TaskACSKey standards to study
Steep turnsPA.V.APOH recommended entry speed: 95 KIAS. Maintain altitude ±100 ft, airspeed ±10 kt, bank angle ±5°, and rollout heading ±10°; maintain coordination. POH 2-9
Ground reference maneuversPA.V.BRectangular course, S-turns across a road, and turns around a point. Maintain altitude ±100 ft and airspeed ±10 kt; correct for wind to achieve the required ground track.

VII. Slow Flight and Stalls

TaskACSKey standards to study
Slow flightPA.VII.AMaintain altitude ±100 ft, heading ±10°, and airspeed +10/−0 kt; maintain coordination and keep bank within the ACS limit (20°).
Power-off stallPA.VII.BUse approach-to-landing configuration; recognize and recover at the first indication or as directed. POH calls for slow deceleration; stall-warning horn sounds about 5-10 kt before the stall in all configurations. Maintain ACS altitude/heading tolerances and recover with coordinated inputs. POH 2-9, 4-42
Power-on stallPA.VII.CUse takeoff/departure configuration; maintain coordination and recover at the first indication or as directed. POH calls for slow deceleration; stall-warning horn sounds about 5-10 kt before the stall in all configurations. Maintain ACS altitude/heading tolerances. POH 2-9, 4-42

VIII. Basic Instrument Maneuvers

TaskACSKey standards to study
Straight-and-level flightPA.VIII.AUse the instrument scan to maintain altitude ±200 ft, heading ±20°, and airspeed ±10 kt.
Constant-airspeed climbsPA.VIII.BMaintain assigned heading within ±10° and airspeed within ±10 kt; level off at the assigned altitude within ±200 ft.
Constant-airspeed descentsPA.VIII.CMaintain assigned heading within ±10° and airspeed within ±10 kt; level off at the assigned altitude within ±200 ft.
Turns to headingsPA.VIII.DMaintain altitude within ±200 ft, use the assigned bank/turn rate, and roll out within ±10° of the assigned heading.
Recovery from unusual flight attitudesPA.VIII.ERecognize nose-high and nose-low unusual attitudes and recover promptly using the proper instrument references and coordinated control inputs.

IX. Emergency Operations

TaskACSKey standards to study
Emergency descentPA.IX.APromptly configure and establish the descent using the aircraft checklist/POH; maintain control, clear the area, and level off at the assigned altitude.
Emergency approach and landing (simulated)PA.IX.BPOH best-glide speed: 68 KIAS. Establish glide promptly, select a suitable landing area, use the checklist and make sound decisions; when practical, meet the ACS touchdown standard, aligned with the landing surface and without side drift. POH Sec. 3, Fig. 3-1
POH note: This card is keyed to the supplied Cessna 172S NAV III NXi POH (172SPHCUS-00); it is a study aid, not a checklist. The POH gives 56 KIAS for short-field obstacle clearance (Sections 4-3 and 4-18), while its VX climb table lists 62 KIAS at sea level and 67 KIAS at 10,000 ft (Section 4-3). Brief this difference with your CFI and use the aircraft's current approved POH/checklist. ACS tolerances are evaluation limits, not targets; the examiner may evaluate other applicable tasks too.
